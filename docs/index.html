How it works

T-1 facts only

Offer, filed range (read from the last S-1/A on EDGAR before the first trade), deal size, venue, and a 90-day regime of listings that closed strictly before. Leakage assertions raise on anything else.

Conformal band

Gradient-boosted 10/50/90 quantiles of log(first close / offer), widened on a later, disjoint window so 80% coverage holds in finite samples (CQR). Trained to 2023, calibrated 2024 on.

A gate that says no

no_range → thin_regime (<5 recent listings) → too_wide (band > 60% of offer). The gate never narrows a band; it decides whether to show one.

Trained to 2022, calibrated on 2023, scored on 428 listings from 2024 to 23 Oct 2026, with filed ranges read from EDGAR for 1,171 of 1,486 deals. The preregistered verdict is reference stands: across every listing the model's band is twice as wide. On the listings the gate publishes, the band is narrower than the reference's, but it under-covers: 68% against an 80% target. So read a published band as tighter than the evidence supports, not as a guarantee. The median's error is 22.6% of the offer, against 23.6% for "it closes at the offer". The gate withheld 325 of 428 listings: 240 as too_wide and 85 as no_range. The calculator above uses the same model refitted for the demo: trained to 2023 and calibrated on 2024 onward. The grey band is the reference: offer × the calibration window's 10th–90th percentile first-day move.
